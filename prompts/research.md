# Stage 1 — Research (factual topics only)

Skip this stage for pure fiction, poems, abstract mood pieces or a script the user supplied (just note
"no research needed" in research.md). For anything factual — news, history, science, health, products,
people, places, statistics — research before writing a word.

## Search
- Run several web searches in parallel (different angles, recent + authoritative). For news: a rolling window
  ending now; breaking first; check publish times.
- Prefer primary sources: official sites, papers, company blogs, government data, museum / encyclopedia pages.
- For a product: its own page (and capture it — screenshots are better than redrawing it).

## Write research.md
- The brief (Stage 0) at the top.
- The facts you will use, each with its source URL; exact numbers and names as the source writes them.
- For news: ~8–12 candidates scored 1–5 on Breaking, Practical value, Impact, Novelty, Visual potential, Audience
  interest → select (breaking first; at least one item with practical value for an ordinary viewer).
- The angle: what is surprising, funny or useful here — the twist the script will land on.
- Picture notes: which facts have a strong visual (a number to count up, a place to map, a logo, a before/after).

## Rules
- Every factual claim needs ≥2 independent sources, or 1 primary source.
- No invented numbers, quotes, dates or opinions presented as fact. Speculation is labelled as such.
- Health / legal / financial topics: general information only, no personal advice; say "check with a professional".
- Skip what can't be told in plain words unless there's an everyday angle.
