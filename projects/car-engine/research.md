# Brief
Explainer · Hebrew · 9:16 · ~20 s · narrator Gemini. How a gasoline car engine works: the four-stroke cycle.

# Facts
- Four strokes: intake (piston down, air-fuel mixture in), compression (piston up, valves closed, mixture squeezed), power (spark ignites it, expansion pushes piston down, turns crankshaft), exhaust (piston up, burnt gases out).
- One cycle = two crankshaft revolutions (720°).
- Power stroke turns the crankshaft that drives the wheels.
