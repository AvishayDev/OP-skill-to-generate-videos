## Facts used
1. Three hearts: two branchial hearts pump blood through the gills, one systemic heart pumps it to the body.
   - https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/ten-wild-facts-about-octopuses-they-have-three-hearts-big-brains-and-blue-blood-7625828/
   - https://www.sciencefocus.com/nature/why-does-an-octopus-have-more-than-one-heart
2. Blue blood: copper-based hemocyanin instead of iron-based hemoglobin.
   - same two sources
3. Nine brains: a central brain plus a cluster of neurons ("mini brain") in each of the eight arms; arms can act independently.
   - https://mirror.triblive.com/lifestyles/more-lifestyles/9-brains-3-hearts-some-wild-facts-about-octopuses (AP)
   - https://www.bristolaquarium.co.uk/blog/10-octopus-facts-you-wont-believe/
