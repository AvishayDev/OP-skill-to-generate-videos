# Five Little Fireflies — brief

Original counting nursery song (5 → 0, in the style of "Five Little Monkeys") for the NuNu TV Nursery Rhymes
channel (YouTube, 16:9, toddlers). A gentle bedtime story: five glowing fireflies in a night meadow fly up to
the moon one by one and become five new stars. Narrator: Leda, sing-song. ~2 min. Every shot painted pixel by
pixel with the image-writer skill (user's choice). No factual claims, so no research needed.
